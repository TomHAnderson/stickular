Frequently Asked Questions

Can I see a picture?

We'd love to feature a picture of your Tracker with Stickular stickers on it!

Will I get a proof?

Yes. Once your order is accepted we'll create a PDF with all your stickers in it and email it to you. We'll wait for your go-ahead and create your custom stickers after your approval.

How long does an order take to arrive?

After you have approved your proof we ship standard mail so expect 7 - 10 business days.

The sticker styles say they are approximations. What does a final sticker look like?

All sticker styles maintain the same border padding while adjusting for special cuts like Label and Battery Notch.

Are the stickers removable?

Yes, but the sticker will be destroyed in the removal process. To clean off any extra sticker stick use Goo Gone. The citrus version of Goo Gone is not effective.
